Ogof Draenen is one of Britain's longest caves, well over 70 km. This survey has about
**49 km** of it: the Oxford University Cave Club's survey data, converted to Therion, with
newer surveys.

## What's here

- **Most of the cave as a Grade 2 survey**: OUCC's centreline, coarse and with long legs (in
  places without clino readings), converted from Survex. It's centreline only, with no
  drawing and no passage walls, so the 3D view shows the shape of the cave, not its size.
- **Newer surveys, drawn in Therion**: Rainbow Canyon (2021) and Tarddiad Pwll Du, the
  second entrance. Tarddiad's tie into the rest of the cave isn't known yet, so it's joined
  provisionally at the foot of the Waterfall Series' first climb, and routes from it start
  there.
- **Names** for the passages, junctions and entrances, for the plan, the 3D view and the
  routes.

## What isn't

**About 20 km of the cave isn't in this data.**
The route planner only knows the surveyed passages, so the way it finds is the shortest
*through the survey*, which may not be the way you'd go, and won't know about a passage
that isn't in it.

The directions are worked out from that coarse centreline: a guide to the shape of the way,
not a description of the passage. Never depend on a phone underground.

## Before a trip

Access to Ogof Draenen is controlled by the
[Pwll Du Cave Management Group](https://pdcmg.org/access/). Take a full survey and a
guidebook, and see the [OUCC's Draenen pages](https://www.oucc.org.uk/surveys/surveys%20and%20data%20-%20draenen/survex/draenen_survex_data.htm)
for the survey data and its history.
